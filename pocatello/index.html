Common Water Damage Issues in Pocatello

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. We have ordered these five common Pocatello calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Shut the house supply, run a tap to drain the lines and phone us immediately, whatever the hour. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. This water has crossed lawns, driveways and streets, which makes it unsanitary. The Coeur d'Alene Restoration team extracts it once conditions are safe, then removes soaked materials that cannot be cleaned.

Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. For Pocatello kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. We extract the water, dry the wood to a safe reading and tell you what we think the source is.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Since growth can start 24 to 48 hours after materials get wet, an old leak that was never fully dried is a likely culprit. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Pocatello.

How Pocatello Homeowners Can Avoid Water Damage

A home usually gives a few hints before a leak turns serious. Our technicians see the same early clues again and again in Pocatello, and they are worth learning. Paint that bubbles or peels, a brown ring on a ceiling, baseboards pulling away from the wall, and flooring that cups or lifts all mean moisture behind the surface. A musty smell in a closet, a toilet that rocks, or a tap that drips belongs on the list too.

Taken singly they are minor, though every one of them gets more expensive with time. Renew the caulk around baths and showers once it splits, because water that slips behind tile stays hidden for a long time. Test a sump pump several times a year by filling the pit until the float trips and the motor runs. Keep stored items off the basement or garage floor on shelves or in plastic bins.

Good upkeep lowers the odds, but it cannot remove them. What it does is make them rarer and smaller, and that matters when the alternative is days of drying equipment in your Pocatello living room.
